# Six bounded frontier studies

This static gallery accompanies the photon-frontier agenda. Open `index.html` through the site's HTTP preview server. Deep links: `#relay`, `#chronolens`, `#scheimpflug`, `#loom`, `#sheets`, `#atlas`. Every study starts paused; **Orbit** is optional. **Prepare image** renders the current frame and offers **Download PNG**. The images intentionally omit the surrounding explanation, so preserve the mode, configuration and scope when reusing them.

`geometry.js` contains deterministic CPU diagnostics; `scene.frag` displays prescribed emission and extinction fields using finite ray quadrature. The RGB palettes are false color. Successful connection polylines come from the actual CPU geometry. Glow widths, material colors, smooth gate widths and exposure are display choices. This is not a complete transport implementation and does not demonstrate unbiasedness, novelty or universal variance reduction.

Subpaths now have a persistent layer: **amber = photon**, **cyan = camera**, **violet = independently proposed medium path**. They remain visible when **Show constraint geometry** is off. World-anchored labels move with the scene, and the ownership strip below the viewport spells out the construction. The inspection camera is distinct from the displayed camera subpath. The PNG export includes the colored geometry; HTML labels and the ownership strip remain page annotations.

Sheets and Relay use a stable viewport fit computed from their minimum, default and maximum parameter settings in both presets. The projection reserves space for the controls, heading and caption; changing a parameter does not recenter or resize the diagram. Camera orbit and zoom remain free after this initial framing. Projection checks cover every subpath vertex at seven desktop/mobile canvas sizes, including the 1140-pixel-wide browser view.

In **Crossed Sheets**, each side has three retained prefix flights followed by two released positive lengths. Their directions are the sheet basis vectors. The full length ranges reconstruct precisely the displayed finite rectangles, including their corners. In the promising case both displayed chains reach the same midpoint on the clipped intersection line; in the failure case their endpoints remain on their separate sheets. Prefix turns indicate earlier scattering, not an evaluated BSDF or surface event. No transport PDF is inferred from this drawing.

In **Scattering Relay**, the violet three-vertex chain is an explicit illustration of the proposed third subpath. Two dashed edges attach its ends to the photon and camera prefixes; the balls belong to the medium proposal. Its vertices are selected from occupied points in the fixed candidate batch. The numerical readouts still evaluate the original **one-vertex** mixture proxy. A joint chain PDF, phase factors, visibility and transport weights are future work; this is neither a completed tridirectional estimator nor a claim of literature novelty. Dim background connections show the one-vertex candidates used for that proxy.

Run `node projects/radiance-lab/primitive-frontier/explorations/check.mjs` from the site root. Checks include plane/support invariants, released-flight reconstruction at sheet corners, positive flight lengths, shared versus disjoint endpoints, medium-chain attachment and occupancy, vector reflection agreement, analytic derivative versus finite differences, aperture-ray coincidence, quadrature stability, exact slab entry, deterministic proposals and matched failure behavior. All six modes are swept through eleven slider positions in both configurations for finite coordinates and the 128-segment upload limit. Shader compilation and visual QA require a WebGL2 browser.

Resolution is capped by pixel budget (950,000 ordinarily, 320,000 for the seven-view aperture study). The volume integral uses 136 midpoint-like jittered steps, or 128 per aperture view. Images are not convergence references. The ray start/end interval and renderer bounding box are finite. Focus-study rotation is bounded, and rays lacking a forward focus-plane intersection retain the background. Motion pauses outside the viewport, on hidden pages, and through the shared portfolio iframe lifecycle; reduced-motion users receive the same static default.
