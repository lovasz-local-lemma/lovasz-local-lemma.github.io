Implementation notes describe the native application. A working experiment, an approximate preview and a general transport estimator establish different things; each document states its own scope.

The useful distinction is whether a feature has a scene renderer, a controlled numerical experiment, or a geometric explanation. A rendered image proves that an execution path ran; it does not by itself prove unbiasedness, completeness or a performance advantage. This ledger records the current boundaries rather than counting every menu item as a completed estimator.

Surface cone plus plane was already implemented

PP33 offers Cone, Plane, and Cone + plane: balance MIS. The pair retains one total sheet budget and accounts for both families on their common emitted-path measure. The retained comparison contains 54 native renders: three scenes, three choices, six independent seeds.

An older running build may still offer only the earlier two choices. Surface-Curve-MIS.bat opens the updated paired caustic scene through the AIO launcher. Choose Sheet family → Cone + plane: balance MIS.

The measured result is also useful: the pair did not provide an efficiency win in the tested fixtures. The two charts have the same marginal emitted-path density; their difference is within-sheet correlation and sweep orientation. The pair provides a controlled orientation hedge, not a new proposal that hits glass more often. Read the derivation and native comparison.

Computed experiments that must not be mistaken for general scene modes

Construction What the earlier experiment actually computed What that did not establish
Sphere × sphere and plane × plane Exact residual circle/line geometry, coarea factors and samples A scene-radiance estimator with source/sensor PDFs, visibility and all path roots.
Ball × parallelepiped and box × box Bounded overlap quadrature A bidirectional path sampler over arbitrary overlapping volumes.
Temporal ellipsoid, ball/box clipping, finite kernel and delta limit A normalized geometric integral and dimension reduction at regular level sets Arrival-time support in the production PP0–33 films. GPU path gates are a separate route.
Surface-footprint derivative A controlled receiver render using a declared smooth edge kernel An infinitely thin boundary derivative of a general moving area light.
FFT wave propagation and hologram record/replay Actual complex-amplitude propagation, intensity-only sideband recovery and refocusing for controlled apertures/layers Coherent multiple scattering through arbitrary scene materials, polarization or physically diffracting in-scene holograms.
Collision, track length and ratio tracking Sample histories, estimates and costs for one specified scalar volume integral Repair or validation of every existing GPU/UPBP medium-transport branch.

These experiments remain useful foundations. The distinction matters when a new drawing looks correct while the corresponding transport estimator has not yet been connected to a scene. The new sphere-connection renderer, sharp boundary derivative and coherent receiver render provide actual bounded scene experiments for those three follow-ups. The earlier support and time experiments and wave laboratory remain alongside them.

Directions still open

  • General transient photon primitives: arrival-time kernels, multisegment prefixes and correctly normalized delta limits are not universally wired through PP0–33. A sphere or ellipsoid picture alone does not complete them.
  • General dual-support transport: arbitrary plane/volume pairings, multiple preimages and manifold constraints still need matching measures, proposals, visibility and variance analysis.
  • Cross-measure MIS: finite-radius beam/ring/arc and kernel-free sheets need an extended derivation; the shipped exact/curve blend does not claim it.
  • General differentiable transport: smooth throughput terms, moving occlusion, refractive interfaces and indirect paths exceed a single boundary fixture or central finite difference.
  • Arbitrary coherent materials and sonar: scalar-wave demonstrations do not supply electromagnetic material boundaries, polarization, acoustic impedance or a transducer model.
  • Full manifold exploration: the existing specular-aware mutation prototype is not the complete constraint solver from the published algorithm. This remains the previously designated lower-priority direction.
  • A universal automatic combination ranking: second moments, support holes, Jacobian tails and cost make the analysis concrete, but there is no scene- independent score that chooses the best combination without assumptions.

The backend and temporal audit and estimator support matrix separate a missing mathematical ingredient from a performance port. A name in the UI, a geometric schematic and an independently checked scene estimator are different kinds of evidence.
