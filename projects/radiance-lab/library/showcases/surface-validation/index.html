More than a new image

Support intersections & time: sphere/sphere circles, plane intersections, solid overlaps and normalized temporal shells. Volume estimators: collision, surviving track length and ratio tracking estimate the same integral with live histories and uncertainty. Light-position derivative: frozen scene measurements with common random samples and changing visibility. Enhanced raster: an opt-in surface-guide/photon composite with explicit approximation limits.
