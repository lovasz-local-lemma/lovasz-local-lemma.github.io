<aside class="scope-note"><span>↔</span><div><strong>A preview is an approximation.</strong><p>Finite angular resolution, screen filtering and a grazing floor introduce bias. The preview uses a pinhole camera and omits fog-sheet contributions seen through camera-side glass or mirrors. More passes reduce sampling noise; they cannot remove these differences. Images establish visible implementation output, not unbiasedness or universal speed.</p></div></aside>
