Curve Atlas

An atlas of how curves are constructed, constrained and intersected. Compare classical splines with local Yuksel interpolation, inspect when exact polynomial constraints produce poor shape, and follow predictor–corrector steps along intersections of implicit surfaces.

The ideaA few control points or constraints can define a smooth curve, but different construction rules produce different shapes. This atlas compares those rules and explores how curves and surfaces meet.

What to watchMove one control and notice which part of the curve changes, then follow the construction or intersection it reveals.
