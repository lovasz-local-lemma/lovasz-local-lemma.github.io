Aperture

A real-time path tracer that treats the camera as an experimental instrument. Compose a tilt-shift photograph, follow wavelength-dependent transport, gate arrivals by travel time and inspect holographic measurements within the same rendering environment.

The ideaA photograph depends on both the light in a scene and how the camera collects it. Aperture simulates that process so focus, wavelength and light travel time become controls you can explore.

What to watchLook for what becomes sharp, changes color or reaches the sensor when a camera or measurement control changes.
