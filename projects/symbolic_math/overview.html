Symbolic Unfolded

A Ruby algebra workbench that searches for a symbolic route through an expression, then attaches evidence to the answer. Substitutions, identities and integration by parts sit alongside residues, exact arithmetic and vector calculus, with expression trees exposing the structure.

The ideaComputer algebra works with formulas as structured objects, looking for exact transformations and answers. This Ruby system searches for ways to integrate or simplify an expression and then checks candidate results.

What to watchFollow the chosen mathematical transformation and the evidence used to check the resulting formula.
