Flatland — Light Transport

Reduce light transport to a plane so individual paths and their weights stay visible. The Ruby/Raylib desktop laboratory has broader inspection tools; the browser runs the corrected Ruby core for PT, direct-light MIS, light tracing and finite-radius photon mapping.

The ideaLight transport follows rays from sources through reflection and scattering to a sensor. Keeping the world two-dimensional makes the paths and the different ways of estimating their contribution visible together.

What to watchFollow a camera path, a light path or a photon gather into the one-dimensional sensor image.
