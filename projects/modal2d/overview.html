Modal2D

Build a musical instrument from its mechanics. Shape, material and supports define a finite-element response; a strike, bow or rub selects its audible modes; then careful manual design or constrained geometry search turns that response into a playable instrument.

The ideaAn object's shape, material and supports determine how it vibrates and therefore how it sounds. Modal2D computes those vibration patterns, turns them into a playable instrument and lets geometry become a tuning control.

What to watchListen for how a different shape or excitation changes the pitch and character of the synthesized sound.
