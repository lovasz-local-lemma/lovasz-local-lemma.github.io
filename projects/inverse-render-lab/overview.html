Inverse Render Lab

Reconstruct a shape, separate appearance from illumination, or recover a hidden target from indirect measurements. Each inverse problem exposes its forward model, fitting variables and residuals, making it possible to distinguish a convincing image from an identifiable physical explanation.

The ideaAn image is evidence of a scene, but different shapes, materials and lights can sometimes explain the same pixels. Inverse rendering searches for a scene that explains measurements and examines what those measurements actually determine.

What to watchCompare the reconstruction with the measurements and the remaining mismatch, not only its final appearance.
