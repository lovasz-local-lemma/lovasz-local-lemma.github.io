Analytical Render · LTC

Replace many direct-light samples with a compact geometric integral. A linearly transformed cosine approximates the material lobe, turning a polygonal emitter into an integrable boundary; a material garage compares the result with Monte Carlo lighting.

The ideaA large light source illuminates a surface from many directions at once. Linearly Transformed Cosines approximate the material's directional response so direct lighting from a polygon can be calculated with a compact geometric integral.

What to watchCompare the smooth calculated lighting with the sampled reference, especially where the material approximation differs.
