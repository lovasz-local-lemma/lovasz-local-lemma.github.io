Spectral geometric optics

Move a prism, lens or mirror and watch separate wavelengths take different paths. This optical bench emphasizes intuition: exaggerated dispersion makes spectral transport, reflection, refraction and total internal reflection easy to follow one interaction at a time.

The ideaDifferent wavelengths of light can bend by different amounts when they enter a material. This optical bench separates them into visible rays so reflection, refraction and total internal reflection can be followed step by step.

What to watchMove an optical element and follow where each wavelength goes; dispersion is exaggerated for clarity.
