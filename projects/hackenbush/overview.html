Hackenbush Lab

Draw a position, cut an edge and inspect the game that remains. Exact finite-game analysis connects colored graphs to canonical forms, component sums, surreal numbers and nimbers, while keeping altered rules and exploratory infinite examples distinct.

The ideaTwo players take turns cutting edges: Blue cuts blue, Red cuts red, and either may cut green; anything disconnected from the ground disappears. Under normal play, the first player with no legal move loses; this lab assigns exact mathematical game values to positions.

What to watchMake one cut and follow both the falling structure and the change in the position's exact game value.
