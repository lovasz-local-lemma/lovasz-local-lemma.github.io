WebGPU Atelier

A WebGPU renderer opened stage by stage, with a wider collection spanning pure mathematics, 3D cellular life, reaction–diffusion, swarm dynamics, forward and inverse physics, and quantum measurement models. Shaders, buffers and intermediate fields connect each result to the computation behind it.

The ideaA graphics effect is built from smaller computations that pass images, geometry and working data to one another. WebGPU Atelier opens those stages and extends the same inspectable approach to mathematical and physical experiments.

What to watchFind the intermediate field or buffer that explains a visible change in the final result.
