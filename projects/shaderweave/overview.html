ShaderWeave

An editable shader graph where intermediate images and previous frames are first-class resources. Compose GLSL passes, preserve feedback state, and connect CUDA or OptiX producers while seeing how a local change propagates through the network.

The ideaA shader is a small program that computes an image or another stage of a graphics effect. ShaderWeave connects these programs as a graph, so each output can feed the next stage or a later frame.

What to watchTrace a connection from an intermediate preview to the final image, especially when previous frames feed back.
