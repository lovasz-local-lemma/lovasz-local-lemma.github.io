What stays editable after export?

RIVX can deliver a baked appearance or keep scene data in a player. This small browser study makes that choice visible: scrub an actual .riv bake, change a live material, then orbit the retained scene.

Light position drives every pane. Roughness and IOR affect the two live shaders; the portable bake keeps its authored materials. Only the retained pane accepts camera movement.

The connection to RIVX

Scene → lighting → marks

  1. Keep scene identities. Spheres, floor and wall are separate regions in the portable bake.
  2. Choose what to evaluate later. The host retains a compact shader and scene parameters for live light, material and camera changes.
  3. Deliver ordinary Rive content. The bake contains image layers and keyed vector marks. The live panes place ordinary Rive contours over host-rendered pixels.

This is a controlled browser explanation of the export boundary. Its dedicated scene schema is not a loader for arbitrary native RIVX projects. Interaction needs no GPU-to-CPU pixel readback; the offline bake utility uses readback to create its images.
