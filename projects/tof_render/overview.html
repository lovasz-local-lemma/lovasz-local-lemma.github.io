Holography & depth reconstruction

Can one spatially coded exposure reveal travel-time phase? This simulation study combines modulated illumination with sensor correlation, isolates a Fourier sideband and reconstructs depth, while showing why carrier placement, shutter timing and phase ambiguity can defeat an apparently plausible setup.

The ideaLight travel time contains information about distance, but a camera needs a way to encode that timing into a measurement. This simulation study uses patterned illumination and phase measurements to reconstruct depth and expose ambiguous readings.

What to watchCompare the true scene with the recovered depth as timing, phase wrapping or multiple light paths change.
