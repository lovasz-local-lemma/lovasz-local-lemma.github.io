LightField / Neural

Move through recorded light, then explore how it breaks apart. Direct ray-field modes produce faithful views, overlapping fragments and surreal layers from the same capture; refocusing and neural models add other ways to carry its appearance.

The ideaA collection of photographs records how a scene looks from different positions and directions. This project reuses those observations to create new viewpoints or focus settings, and compares direct reuse with learned scene representations.

What to watchMove away from the captured viewpoints and notice where the scene stays coherent or separates into seams and layers.
