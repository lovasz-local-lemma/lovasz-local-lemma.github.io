Catalan structures

Trees, parentheses, lattice paths and triangulations share a recursive structure. This focused combinatorics study makes their correspondences concrete and uses the reflection principle to show exactly which paths a Catalan count excludes.

The ideaBalanced parentheses, certain lattice paths, trees and polygon triangulations can encode the same recursive choices. A bijection translates each valid object into exactly one object of another kind, explaining why their counts agree.

What to watchFollow one object's choices through the different representations and see which paths the boundary excludes.
