Integral Atlas · A guide to tricky integrals

A work-in-progress notebook for studying tricky integrals. Start with a worked example, identify the useful move and inspect a contour when it helps explain the argument. Integral Bee supplied the original puzzle; the focus is now on a smaller set of reusable ideas.

The ideaA difficult integral can become simpler after a useful change of viewpoint, such as symmetry, substitution or a path through the complex plane. This notebook works through examples and makes selected constructions visible.

What to watchIdentify the step that makes the integral easier and the assumptions that allow that step.
