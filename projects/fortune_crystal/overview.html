Fortune’s Loom

Follow Fortune and Apollonius geometry through the events, search structures and arithmetic decisions that create the diagram. Degenerate cases and precision policies remain visible; the 3D sweep extension is explicitly an unfinished exploration.

The ideaA Voronoi diagram divides space according to which point is nearest; related diagrams extend the question to disks. Fortune's sweep constructs these boundaries through a moving frontier and a sequence of geometric events.

What to watchSee which event changes the frontier and how that change creates or removes a boundary.
