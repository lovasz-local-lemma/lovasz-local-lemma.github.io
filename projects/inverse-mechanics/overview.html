Inverse Mechanics & Optics

Choose an outcome and search for the physical parameters that produce it: a kick that reaches target poses, a lens that forms a caustic image, or threads that resolve from a chosen viewpoint. Forward simulation supplies the evidence for each proposed design.

The ideaForward physics predicts what happens after a push; inverse physics searches for the push that reaches a chosen outcome. The same idea can search for an optical shape that creates a desired light pattern.

What to watchCompare the requested destination or image with the simulated result as the search changes its design.
