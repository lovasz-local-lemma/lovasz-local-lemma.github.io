Drawing with harmonics

A drawing is a timed performance, not only an outline. Keep a gesture's changing speed and inspect its moving STFT spectrum, then compare global Fourier selection, cosine symmetry, pinned-end sine modes and local wavelet detail through their reconstructions.

The ideaA drawn motion can be decomposed into simpler oscillations or local pieces and rebuilt from selected components. This project compares those decompositions while preserving the timing of the gesture.

What to watchRemove or restore components and notice which details of the path and its timing return.
