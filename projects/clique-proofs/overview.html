Clique Proofs

A growing educational atlas of direct proof routes. Compactness, minimum spanning trees and Fourier-related clusters connect apparently different formulations of one concept; each bridge exposes assumptions and structure that a bare equivalence statement can hide.

The ideaTwo mathematical statements can describe the same underlying property while suggesting very different proofs. This atlas connects equivalent viewpoints through direct arguments and makes their required assumptions explicit.

What to watchFollow one proof route and compare what another route reveals about the same idea.
