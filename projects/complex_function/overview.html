Complex functions

See a complex function as geometry: how it moves a region, changes local scale and wraps around singularities. Branch structure and Riemann surfaces give a visible vocabulary for contour integration, phase and multivalued functions.

The ideaA complex number is a point with two coordinates, and a complex function moves those points to new positions. Watching an entire region move reveals stretching, rotation, singularities and the different branches of multivalued functions.

What to watchTrack a recognizable part of the original region into its image, especially near a singularity or branch.
