Graph algorithms

Inspect the working state behind a graph result. Lazy and eager Prim expose different queue bookkeeping, SPFA reveals its changing frontier, and Floyd–Warshall retains path witnesses. Assumptions, updates and counterexamples explain subtleties that a final highlighted graph would hide.

The ideaA graph represents objects as vertices and relationships as edges, often with costs. These algorithms answer questions such as how to connect every vertex cheaply or find short routes, while exposing the bookkeeping that leads to the answer.

What to watchFollow the queue or distance update that changes the current candidate solution.
