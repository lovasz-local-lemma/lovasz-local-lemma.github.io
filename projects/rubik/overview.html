Advanced Rubik & Variant Studio

A puzzle studio where a move can change which moves are legal next. Begin with Latch Cube, then compare directional locks, bandages, coupled turns and reactive variants through synchronized 3D puzzles, flat nets and local route graphs.

The ideaA twisty puzzle is a collection of states connected by legal turns. These variants add locks, joined pieces or coupled moves, so making a turn can change which turns are available next.

What to watchAfter each move, compare the puzzle, its flat net and the newly available routes.
