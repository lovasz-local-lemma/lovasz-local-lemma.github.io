Circle · Roundness Lab

What should a good circle score actually measure? Draw a contour, separate size from shape, and compare radial error, annulus width, compactness, curvature and harmonics. Counterexamples expose where a convenient aggregate score hides a visible defect.

The ideaA hand-drawn loop can resemble a circle in several different ways, so one score may miss an obvious defect. This lab measures distance, curvature and other shape properties and compares what each score rewards.

What to watchDraw a deliberate imperfection and see which measurements detect it and which overlook it.
