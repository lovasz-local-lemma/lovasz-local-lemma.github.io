Equations and model boundary
ρ/ρ₀ = 1/J;  pliquid = K max(1/J − 1, 0)
Wbulk = K(J − ln J − 1)
Wsolid = Wbulk + μ ‖F − R‖²

A constitutive-response comparison, not a time-stepping SPH/MPM benchmark. R is the polar rotation of F; rigid rotation adds no elastic energy. The displayed Wbulk is the solid bulk energy. For the liquid, it is clamped to zero at J≥1, matching the absence of tensile pressure. Shape energy includes elastic stretch as well as shear. Liquid viscosity and surface tension are omitted to isolate static response. The independent-point control has neither a density law nor stored strain; pairwise contacts alone do not supply either. SPH and MPM are numerical representations for continuum laws, not substitutes for choosing those laws.
