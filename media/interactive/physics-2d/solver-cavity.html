Equations and model boundary
pV = mRT;  U = m cᵥT
ṁ = −q;  U̇ = Q − Qcool − q cₚTupstream
q = conductance × (p − pamb)

Dimensionless, fixed-volume ideal gas: R=1, γ=1.4, initial p=T=m=V=1. Heat is supplied until t=3. Signed q permits ambient inflow; upstream temperature is the chamber temperature for outflow and ambient temperature for inflow. The opening time is prescribed; no fracture, spatial jet, combustion or shock is simulated. The native sandbox instead couples cellwise pressure surrogates, reachability masks and authored vent responses. This experiment explains why topology, release rate and energy accounting are distinct requirements; it is not a native pressure validation.
