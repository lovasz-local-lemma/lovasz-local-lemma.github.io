03 / Compose the field

Can we keep useful structure across hidden dimensions? Combine procedural field generators, then explore how axis mapping and orientation change the 3D slice. This complements the inverse problem: projections constrain what must be present; a generative field controls what happens between those constraints.

Inverse constraints versus procedural continuity

More dimensions supply more degrees of freedom, but they do not automatically produce a useful animation. Periodic fields can support looping playback; balanced treatment of coordinates can avoid privileging one slice direction. Neither property implies strict rotational isotropy or compatibility with arbitrary target shadows.
