## GGX Linearly Transformed Cosine Lookup Data

`ltc-data.js` includes half-float encodings of the GGX LTC lookup tables from
the three.js `RectAreaLightTexturesLib`, which identifies the original data as
derived from Eric Heitz's `ltc_code` reference implementation.

- https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/jsm/lights/RectAreaLightTexturesLib.js
- https://github.com/selfshadow/ltc_code
