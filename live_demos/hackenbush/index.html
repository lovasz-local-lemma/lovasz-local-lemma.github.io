You move first

A small graph can hide a surprisingly subtle game. Cut an edge, watch unsupported branches fall, and inspect the exact game value behind the move. Some positions are surreal numbers; others need the richer language of short games.

First visit · from a move to its mathematical explanation
  1. Choose a small position from Numbers or Nimbers. Compare a red–blue number with a green impartial game.
  2. Use Play to test a move, or Create to change one edge. Follow the board's cut rules and compare what remains.
  3. Switch to Create for Position Analysis, then turn on Live reasoning. Compare the options with the reduced game form, then try a sum of components.

Finite positions are solved exactly when they fit the search budget. Stable anchors are a nonstandard extension; infinite and loopy references have separately stated support. What the engine supports →
