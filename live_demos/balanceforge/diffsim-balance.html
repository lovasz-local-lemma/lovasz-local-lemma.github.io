LQR (analytic). Reads the linearized dynamics straight out of the differentiable simulator (finite- difference at the true inverted fixed point), then solves the discrete Riccati equation for a state-feedback gain u = -K·x. Provably asymptotically stable near upright → it holds indefinitely (verified 30s, no fall, from any small tilt). This is how real multi-pendulum rigs do it. Engine: js/diffsim_lqr.js (proof: diffsim-lqr-probe.js).
Neural (exact gradients). Trains a small feedback MLP live by Adam backprop THROUGH the simulator — watch the "best hold" climb. But a memoryless policy trained on a short (~1.5s, near-linear) horizon has a finite basin: it holds cleanly ~1.5–2s, so this loops fresh catches. Same physics, learned rather than solved. Engine: js/diffsim_trainer.js (diffsim-trainer-probe.js).
