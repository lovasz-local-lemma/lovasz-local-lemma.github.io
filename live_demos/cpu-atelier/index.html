Plume representation

More ray samples refine smoke and hot emission at extra rendering cost. The fluid grid and timestep stay fixed.

Voxel splats are compact 3D supports sampled from the same smoke and temperature fields. They rotate with the scene and are occluded by its surfaces. This stylizes the plume; surfaces remain path traced.

A coarser representation can reduce volume-rendering work, but does not accelerate the 48³ simulation. Compare the live stage timings; image quality and density coverage also change.

Model & measurement scope

This is a CPU systems study with illustrative transport and materials. The volume uses single scattering with a heuristic ambient fill; rough metal and clearcoat are simplified shading models.

Cloth uses projected distance constraints. Its fluid coupling combines drag with moving grid obstacles, rather than a fully momentum-conserving joint solver. Each fresh image follows one fixed 0.1 s simulation update; presentation is not real-time physical pacing. Quality presets rebuild cloth at a different tessellation while retaining the 48³ fluid. Smooth adapts only the image resolution.

The startup self-check samples a renderer tile and a seeded fluid update. The complete engine frame runs in a dedicated worker, leaving controls responsive while the CPU works. Expensive scenes can still produce fewer fresh images.
